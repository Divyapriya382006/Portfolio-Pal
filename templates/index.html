How Portfolio Pal Works

1. Create a Profile

Build your portfolio effortlessly using simple forms. No coding needed, no stress involved. Just enter your details and watch your very own portfolio come to life.

2. View Your Portfolio

Take a peek at everything you’ve entered. View your entire database anytime so you always know what information your portfolio is built on.

3. View Other's Portfolio

If you're curious about how others designed their portfolios, all you need is their username and visitor key to explore their work safely without any edit access. And when it's your turn to share, simply give out your own visitor key to friends, peers or recruiters — they'll be able to view your portfolio securely while all your data stays protected and uneditable.

4. Delete Your Account and Data

Changed your mind or want a fresh start? You can remove your whole account and all database entries in one click. And yes, don’t worry, we’ll still ask “Are you sure?” before anything drastic happens.

5. Switch to Light Mode and Dark Mode

Whether you prefer bright and vibrant screens or calm late-night vibes, the website instantly adapts to your mood with a theme switcher.
